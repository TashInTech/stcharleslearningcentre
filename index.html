A word from the director

Dear friends and partners,

Greetings from St. Charles Learning Centre. We are a humble school with a big dream: to provide quality education, hope, and a brighter future for vulnerable children in Kamarya Village, Kiruhura District.

Our children face major challenges, including limited classroom space, poor learning conditions, and the daily struggle for basic opportunities. Yet they continue to show resilience, curiosity, and determination. We believe that education is the key that can break the cycle of poverty and unlock a better tomorrow.

Beyond the walls of our school, these children navigate daily obstacles that would break the spirit of many. Some walk over 5 kilometers every morning to attend classes, while others study after laboring in fields. Despite these hardships, their commitment to learning never wavers. They remind us every single day why our mission is so critical and why we must succeed.

The potential within these children is extraordinary—they are curious, ambitious, and dreaming of futures as teachers, doctors, engineers, and leaders. What they lack is not ambition but resources and opportunity. A permanent classroom block would transform their learning environment from a leaky wooden shelter to a safe, dignified space where they can focus entirely on their studies.

We therefore appeal to your compassion and generosity. Whether you are an individual, business, church, or organization, your support can directly change the trajectory of children's lives. Your contribution helps us complete our classroom construction, provide quality learning materials, offer nutritious meals, and create the safe, conducive environment these deserving children deserve.

Every donation, no matter the size, brings us closer to this vision. By investing in St. Charles Learning Centre, you are not simply building classrooms—you are building futures, breaking cycles of poverty, and transforming an entire community for generations to come. Together, we can turn these dreams into lasting reality.

About us

More about our school

We are a private mixed day primary school that believes in creating opportunities and empowering young people through education. St.Charles Learning Centre was established in 2022 with a goal of providing basic primary education to needy children in Kamarya Village, Kiruhura District, in Western Uganda. The school is registered under the Uganda Ministry of Education and Sports.

St.Charles Learning Centre currently has an enrollment of 102 children — 52 girls and 50 boys — supported by 22 teachers and staff. Lessons are taught in a temporary wooden shelter while a permanent classroom block is under construction to give pupils a safe, weather-resistant space to learn.

Our flagship ongoing project is the Classroom Block Construction Project — replacing the wooden shelter that floods during rains with a permanent block that will also serve as a venue for community training and youth empowerment workshops.

Our greatest challenges

Why we need your support

🏫

Classroom Shortage

Our 102 pupils currently study under a wooden shelter that floods during rainy season. We urgently need permanent classrooms to provide a safe, dignified learning environment.

📚

Learning Materials

Limited textbooks, exercise books, and educational resources mean students struggle to keep up with lessons. Quality materials are essential for effective learning and academic success.

🍽️

Student Nutrition

Many children come to school on empty stomachs after helping with farm work. Nutritious meals would improve concentration, health, and academic performance significantly.

👨‍🏫

Teacher Welfare

Our 22 dedicated teachers work with minimal resources and low compensation. Better teacher support ensures quality instruction and reduces staff turnover in our school.
